Offline Mode

Play Duck Race anytime, anywhere — even without an internet connection.

Play Duck Race Anytime, Anywhere

Duck Race now works offline! We've implemented Progressive Web App (PWA) technology to ensure you can enjoy Duck Race even when you don't have an internet connection.

How It Works

  • Duck Race automatically saves essential game resources to your device
  • When you're offline, the app uses these saved resources
  • You'll see a notification when you're in offline mode
  • Once you're back online, you'll automatically reconnect

Benefits

  • Play Duck Race without an internet connection
  • Faster loading times for frequently used features
  • Reduced data usage
  • More reliable experience in areas with poor connectivity

Installing Duck Race as an App

For the best offline experience, you can install Duck Race as an app on your device:

On Chrome (Desktop)

  1. Click the install icon (⊕) in the address bar
  2. Select "Install Duck Race"
  3. The app will open in its own window

On Safari (iOS)

  1. Tap the share button
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

On Chrome (Android)

  1. Tap the menu button (three dots)
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  3. Tap "Add" to confirm

Limitations in Offline Mode

While most features work offline, there are some limitations:

  • You cannot create or join online multiplayer rooms
  • User profile and subscription information may not be up-to-date
  • New content or updates won't be available until you reconnect

Don't worry! The core Duck Race game works perfectly offline, so you can still enjoy racing ducks anytime!
