How to Use the Wheel of Names

Using our random name picker is simple and intuitive. Follow these easy steps:

1. Add Names

Enter the names of participants in the list or paste them from a spreadsheet

2. Spin the Wheel

Click the "Spin" button or use the keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Enter)

3. Get Your Winner

The wheel stops on a random name and announces the winner

4. Track History

View previous winners and continue spinning for multiple selections

Pro Tip: You can customize the wheel by adding as many names as you need. The wheel automatically adjusts to accommodate your list!
